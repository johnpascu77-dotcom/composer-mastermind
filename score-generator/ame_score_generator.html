AME Score Generator

A Composer Mastermind score for the romanian_shadow style profile: one Atonal Motive Transformer per voice, each on its own MIDI channel. Choose which chain each voice enters every step (two bars by default), then download the score and open it in Mastermind (Score View → Load Score...). Everything happens in this page; nothing is uploaded.

1 · Your AME presets

AME numbers its presets: the 7 factory ones, then every preset of your folder sorted by name. The score needs those numbers, so point this at your AME preset folder — usually %APPDATA%\AtonalMotiveEngine\ChainPresets (paste that into the folder dialog's address bar). The names are remembered for next time. Install the profile first (profiles\romanian_shadow\make_presets.py --install).

3 · The plan — what each voice plays, one step at a time

How long each cell lasts. A motif longer than a step overlaps the next entrance, so a long motif wants a longer step (or rests).

Each cell is one step (the length you set above): pick a preset, or — rest — (the voice gets no new entrance and lets its last one finish). Chains and entrances are sent one bar ahead, so they land exactly on the bar line.

Generate a plan builds a whole new plan from a seed: the same form, seed and restlessness always give the same plan. Restlessness is how often voices play rather than rest, and how far a movement strays from its own family of presets. Then edit any cell by hand.
